Stoolball

A full innings on placeholder figures. You bat; they bowl, keep and field. Every figure on screen is drawn from the sprite list, so what you see here is what the batch run has to produce.

HOW TO BAT

  1. Move the pointer up and down over the pitch to raise and lower the bat. Up/Down arrows work too. Facing, it rests at board height.
  2. Press (click, tap, Space) to swing at that height. One swing per ball.
  3. Once it is hit into the field, press to run. Press again while running to call for another. The throw comes in from the right to whichever end you are in trouble at.
  4. The field map top right shows it from above: fielders in red, the one chasing in pink, your batters in amber, the ball in white. The wicket the throw is going to flashes.
  5. Short of your ground when the board is hit, and you are run out. Overs alternate between women and men bowlers.
