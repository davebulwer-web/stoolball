# Title screen

`assets/title.png` (320x180) is Pixellab pixflux art (1 generation, prompt: a paddle bat and ball lying in the grass by a board-on-a-post wicket, sunset, village green, no text).
The notice lines Pixellab drew on the board were painted out (a wicket board is plain), and the title was painted on by script with DejaVu Serif Bold at 30px, no anti-aliasing, chalk STOOL and amber BALL with a dark outline and shadow.
`assets/icon.png` is the paddle head and ball cropped from the same art.
